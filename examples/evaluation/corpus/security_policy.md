# Security and retention policy
Customer financial records must be retained for 7 years.
Application logs must be retained for 90 days; diagnostic debug logs must be retained for 14 days.
Encryption uses AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
Emergency administrator access expires after 60 minutes and requires a recorded justification.
The policy names encryption standards but never includes encryption keys, passwords, or access tokens.
