# Northstar leave policy — effective 1 January 2025
Full-time employees receive 20 paid leave days per calendar year.
Up to 5 unused days may carry over; carried days expire on 31 March of the following year.
Leave requests of more than 5 consecutive working days require manager approval at least 14 calendar days in advance.
This version is superseded by the policy effective 1 January 2026.
