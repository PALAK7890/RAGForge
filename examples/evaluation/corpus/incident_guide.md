# Incident operations guide
Payments API outage INC-042 is classified as severity P1.
The service owner for Payments API is the Commerce team.
During a P1 incident, the incident commander posts status updates every 30 minutes.
The escalation contact for Commerce is the on-call Commerce engineer, reached through the internal paging system.
After service restoration, the incident commander must publish a review within 3 business days.
Do not restart the database before preserving diagnostic logs.
