# Northstar support commitments
Premium customers receive 24/7 support. Standard customers receive support Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 UTC, excluding public holidays.
For Premium customers, severity P1 has a first-response target of 15 minutes and a restoration target of 4 hours.
For Standard customers, severity P1 has a first-response target of 2 business hours; no restoration target is committed.
Severity P2 for Premium customers has a first-response target of 1 hour.
A response target means acknowledging the ticket; it is not a guarantee that the issue will be resolved.
