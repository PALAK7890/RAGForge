# Fictional product catalog
Atlas Basic costs USD 19 per workspace per month and includes 3 seats.
Atlas Pro costs USD 49 per workspace per month and includes 10 seats.
Extra seats cost USD 6 per seat per month on either plan.
Atlas Pro includes audit-log export; Atlas Basic does not.
Atlas Basic and Atlas Pro both include email support under the Standard support tier.
Premium support is sold separately; its price is not disclosed in this catalog.
